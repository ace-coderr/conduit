# Getting Started

Get up and running with Conduit in under 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Open Conduit

Go to [conduitpay.xyz](https://conduitpay.xyz) in your browser. No download required.

---

## Step 2 — Connect or create a wallet

You have two options:

**Option A — Seedless wallet (recommended for beginners)**
Click **Connect Wallet** and choose email, Google, or passkey. A wallet is created for you automatically. No MetaMask, no seed phrase.

**Option B — MetaMask**
Click **Connect Wallet** and choose MetaMask. Make sure you're on Arc Testnet (Chain ID: 5042002).

---

## Step 3 — Get testnet USDC

Conduit runs on Arc Testnet. You need testnet USDC to make payments.

1. Go to [faucet.circle.com](https://faucet.circle.com)
2. Select **Arc Testnet**
3. Paste your wallet address
4. Click **Request** — USDC arrives in under a minute

---

## Step 4 — Create your first payment link

1. Click **Links** in the navigation
2. Click **Create Link**
3. Set a title (e.g. "Freelance Invoice #1") and amount (e.g. 10 USDC)
4. Toggle **Stealth Mode** on if you want privacy
5. Click **Create**
6. Copy the link and share it with your payer

---

## Step 5 — Add your email (optional)

Go to **Account → Profile** and add your email address to receive payment notifications.

You'll get an email every time:
- Someone pays your link
- Your escrow is funded
- A dispute is raised
- A marketplace listing is approved

---

## Step 6 — Claim your username (optional)

Go to **Account → Profile** and set a @username.

Your personal pay page will be live at `conduitpay.xyz/u/yourname` — share it instead of your wallet address.

---

## Add Arc Testnet to MetaMask

If you're using MetaMask, add Arc Testnet manually:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Network name | Arc Testnet |
| RPC URL | `https://rpc.testnet.arc.network` |
| Chain ID | `5042002` |
| Currency symbol | `USDC` |
| Block explorer | `https://testnet.arcscan.app` |
