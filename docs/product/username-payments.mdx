Claim a @username and get a personal payment page at `conduitpay.xyz/u/yourname`. Share your username instead of a wallet address.

## Why usernames matter

Wallet addresses are 42 random characters. Usernames are human.

| Without username | With username |
|-----------------|---------------|
| `0x8557FABdC62f59A1bA7D6...` | `@ace` |
| A long string nobody remembers | A handle people remember |
| Exposes your real wallet | Private — your wallet stays hidden |

## How to claim your username

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Go to **Account → Profile** and connect your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a username">
    Letters, numbers, and underscores only — 3 to 20 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign to verify">
    Sign the message to prove you own the wallet, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your pay page is immediately live at `conduitpay.xyz/u/yourname`.

## Username rules

- Letters, numbers, and underscores only
- 3 to 20 characters
- Unique — first come, first served
- Case insensitive
- Can be changed any time

<Info>
Your username is separate from your wallet address. Changing it does not affect your wallet or existing payment links.
</Info>
