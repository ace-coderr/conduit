Conduit is a payments infrastructure built on Arc Network, powered by Circle USDC. It lets anyone send and receive digital dollars instantly — without a bank account, without KYC, and without complicated setup.

Conduit is designed to be a universal payment layer for people, businesses, developers, and autonomous AI systems.

<Note>
Create a link → someone pays → you receive USDC instantly. That's it.
</Note>

## The one-line summary

Conduit turns your wallet into a payment page — private, escrowed, and x402-powered.

## What makes Conduit different

| Traditional crypto payments | Conduit |
|----------------------------|---------|
| Share wallet address publicly | Share a link or @username |
| Payer picks the right network | Works from any chain automatically |
| No protection for P2P trades | Escrow with Circle-managed custody |
| No way for AI agents to pay | x402 — agents pay automatically |
| Seed phrase required | Sign in with email or Google |
