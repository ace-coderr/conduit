title: "Split Payments"

Split Payments let one incoming USDC payment automatically distribute to up to 10 wallets, by percentage. Perfect for revenue shares, team payouts, creator collaborations, and affiliate splits.

## How it works

1. **Create a split** — set a title, total amount, and the recipients with their percentages (must sum to 100%).
2. **Share the pay link** — anyone can pay it in one transaction.
3. **Funds settle into a Circle wallet** — Conduit provisions a dedicated wallet for the split.
4. **Auto-distribution** — each recipient's share is sent to them automatically, gaslessly, on Arc.

The payer pays once. Every recipient gets their exact cut, instantly.

## Key details

- Up to **10 recipients** per split
- Percentages must total **100%**
- Distribution is **gasless** (via Circle Gas Station)
- Payouts are **resumable** — if a leg fails, retrying skips already-paid recipients
- The last recipient absorbs any rounding remainder so totals always reconcile

## Use cases

- **Revenue sharing** — split income between partners automatically
- **Team payroll** — pay your whole team from one payment
- **Creator collabs** — a song, article, or project splits to every contributor
- **Affiliate payouts** — split a sale between seller and referrer

<Info>
Splits appear in your Transactions page under the Splits tab, with full payout status per recipient.
</Info>
