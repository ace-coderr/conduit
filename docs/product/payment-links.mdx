A payment link is a shareable URL that lets anyone pay you USDC instantly. No wallet address sharing. No network selection. One link, one click, done.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a link">
    Set a title and USDC amount at conduitpay.xyz.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Copy the URL and send it via DM, email, invoice, or social media.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payer clicks and pays">
    They open the link, connect their wallet, and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You receive USDC">
    Funds land in your wallet in under half a second.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key properties

| Property | Details |
|----------|---------|
| One-time use | Each link can only be paid once |
| Optional expiry | Link becomes inactive after a set date |
| Multi-chain | Payer can be on any supported chain |
| Stealth mode | Optional — hides your real wallet address |
| Fee | 0.5% per payment, deducted automatically |

## After payment

- Link status changes to **COMPLETED**
- The transaction appears in your **Transactions** page
- If you've added your email, you receive a payment notification
- Funds are in your wallet instantly
