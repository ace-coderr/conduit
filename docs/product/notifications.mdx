title: "Email Notifications"
description: "Automatic email alerts for every payment event."
---

Conduit sends automatic email notifications for every payment event. Add your email in your profile settings to activate them.

## Notification events

| Event | Who gets notified |
|-------|------------------|
| Payment link paid | Seller |
| Escrow funded by buyer | Seller |
| Escrow funds released | Buyer |
| Dispute raised | Seller |
| Escrow refunded | Buyer |
| Marketplace listing approved | Developer |
| Marketplace listing rejected | Developer |

## How to activate

Go to **Account → Profile**, enter your email address, and save (you'll sign to verify ownership). Notifications are active immediately.

## Sent from

All emails are sent from `noreply@conduitpay.xyz` with reply-to `conduitpay@gmail.com`. If you reply to a notification email, your message goes to the Conduit team.

<Info>
Email is completely optional. Conduit works fully without it — adding your email only activates notifications.
</Info>
