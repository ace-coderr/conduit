Conduit keeps you informed the moment money moves. Get notified by **email** and **Telegram** for every payment, escrow, and split event.

## Notification events

| Event | Who gets notified |
|-------|------------------|
| Payment link paid | Seller |
| Escrow funded by buyer | Seller |
| Escrow funds released | Seller |
| Dispute raised | Seller |
| Escrow refunded | Buyer |
| Split funded | Creator |
| Split distributed | Creator |

## Telegram notifications

Get an instant message the moment you're paid — no refreshing a dashboard.

**To connect:**
1. Go to **Account → Profile**
2. Tap **Connect Telegram**
3. Press **Start** in the bot (`@ConduitPayAppBot`)

Your wallet links automatically, and the page updates once connected. Each alert shows the amount and a link to the on-chain transaction. Disconnect anytime from your profile.

## Email notifications

Add your email in your profile to receive payment receipts and important alerts. Emails are sent from `noreply@conduitpay.xyz`.

## How to activate

Go to **Account → Profile**, add your email and/or connect Telegram, and save (you'll sign to verify wallet ownership).
